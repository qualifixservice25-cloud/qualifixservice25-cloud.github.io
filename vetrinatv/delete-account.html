Delete your VetrinaTV account

You can ask at any time for your account and all linked data to be deleted. Here is how.

VetrinaTVDeveloper: Qualifix Service by Jeffrey Andreatta

How to request deletion

  1. If you have a subscription, cancel it first.Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription on Google Play. Open it from Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › VetrinaTV › Cancel subscription.
  2. Write to us from your account email.Press the button below: a pre-filled email opens. Send the request from the address you use to sign in to VetrinaTV.
  3. We verify and delete.We reply to the same address to confirm the request is yours, then delete everything within 30 days (usually much sooner) and let you know once it is done.

Write the request

Lost access to that email? Write to us anyway at qualifix.service25@gmail.com: we will ask a few questions to check the account is yours.

What gets deleted

  • the sign-in account (email and identifier);
  • your business and its settings;
  • all slides and playlists;
  • all uploaded photos, including the files;
  • linked screens: the TVs go back to the pairing-code screen;
  • the subscription status inside VetrinaTV.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, for as long as tax and accounting obligations require.
  • Technical crash reports (without your content), which Firebase Crashlytics deletes by itself after a limited period.

Don't want to delete everything?

You can also remove only part of your data, directly in the app:

  • A single screen: Screens › tap the screen › Remove screen.
  • Slides: Content › swipe a slide to the left to delete it.
  • Photos: when choosing a photo, "Photos already uploaded" › Delete the photo. Photos you no longer use are deleted automatically after 7 days.

To know what data we process and why, read the Privacy policy.
