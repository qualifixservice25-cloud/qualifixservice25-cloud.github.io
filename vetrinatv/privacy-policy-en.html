Privacy policy

How we handle data when you use VetrinaTV, the Qualifix Service app that shows menus and offers on your shop's TV.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short VetrinaTV only processes the data it needs to work: your email, the content you write (texts, prices, photos) and the screen's status. There are no ads, we don't sell data and we don't profile anyone. You can ask for everything to be deleted at any time: how to do it.

1. Data controller

Qualifix Service by Jeffrey Andreatta, Levico Terme (TN), Italy.
Email: qualifix.service25@gmail.com

For any question about your data, write to this address.

2. Who the app is for

VetrinaTV is made for business owners (shops, cafés, pizzerias, bakeries, workshops…) who want to show menus and offers on a TV. It is not aimed at children. The app does not use the TV's camera or microphone and does not collect data about people looking at the screen or about your shop's customers.

App permissions

  • Internet and network state: to sync content and send the heartbeat.
  • Camera (phone only): used only to read the TV pairing QR. Images are neither saved nor sent.
  • Phone photos: the app only receives the photos you choose, to upload them.
  • Start at boot and "Display over other apps" (TV only): used to restart the app by itself after a power-off or standby.

4. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service and managing your account and subscription: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security, abuse prevention, diagnostics and improving the app: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Tax and accounting obligations: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do no marketing or profiling, so we don't ask for consent for it. There are no ads in the app.

5. Who can see the data

To run VetrinaTV we rely on providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics): stores accounts, content and photos. The database and functions are in the Milan region (europe-west8).
  • Google Play: handles purchases and subscriptions.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: manages subscription status (it receives your customer identifier and purchase information).

We don't sell data and we don't share it with anyone for advertising. We may disclose it to authorities only where the law requires.

Whoever runs the service (Qualifix Service) can technically access the storage for support, security and maintenance. We do so only when needed, for example to answer one of your requests, and we don't look at your photos and content for any other purpose.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. In those cases transfers rely on the tools provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and content: as long as you have the account. When you ask for deletion we delete them.
  • Unused photos: if a photo is not used by any slide, we delete it automatically after 7 days. You can also delete it straight away from the app.
  • Pairing codes: they expire after 15 minutes and are deleted.
  • Screen status: overwritten at every heartbeat; we keep only the latest one.
  • Diagnostics: for the period set by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Purchases: records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are kept for as long as their rules and tax obligations require.

8. Your rights

At any time you can ask us to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, receive it in a readable format (portability) or object to its processing. Write to qualifix.service25@gmail.com: we reply within one month.

If you believe your data is not being handled properly you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of your country.

9. Deleting your account

You can ask for your account and all linked data to be deleted from the Delete account page.

10. Security

  • All communication between app, TV and servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each business only sees its own data; each TV only sees the data of the account it is linked to.
  • The service-menu PIN is stored as a cryptographic fingerprint and, after 5 wrong attempts, the TV locks for a few minutes.

11. Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect children's data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change something important we update this page and the date above. When a change directly affects you we tell you in the app or by email.

13. Contact
